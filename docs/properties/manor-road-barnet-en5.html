Viewing & surveyor checklist

  • Incoming Water Supply: Inspect pipe beneath kitchen sink/stopcock. Look for 25mm/32mm blue MDPE pipe (upgraded) vs dull grey lead pipe (restricted pressure).
  • Hot Water & Showers: Locate the cylinder. Confirm if there is an unvented Megaflo cylinder delivering mains-pressure showers, or an old gravity-fed header tank in the attic.
  • Consumer Unit & Electricals: Open the fuse box. Verify whether it is a modern 18th-edition metal enclosure with RCBOs, Type 2 Surge Protection (SPD), and a 100A main fuse.
  • Underfloor Heating Manifold: If UFH is claimed, ask to see the hydraulic mixing manifold. A wall thermostat alone often indicates cheap, high-running-cost electric matting.
  • Subfloor & Thermal Draughts: Walk the ground floor perimeter. Feel skirtings and air vents for draughts indicating uninsulated suspended timber voids.
  • Roof Sub-structure: Inspect the loft space. Check for breathable felt (e.g. Tyvek) and at least 270mm of mineral wool insulation vs rotting timber lath and torching.
  • Structural Alterations & RSJs: For opened-up spaces or removed chimney breasts, request the structural engineer's calculations and Building Regulations Completion Certificate.
  • Drainage & Build-Over: If extended to the rear, verify if a Thames Water Build-Over Agreement was executed for any public sewers crossing the plot.
  • Garden Solar Path & Boundary Shadows: Check the rear garden with a sun-tracking compass; check if neighbouring two- or three-storey gables cast permanent flank shadows.
  • Side Access Rights: Confirm uninterrupted private title access from front to back garden without crossing third-party land.
